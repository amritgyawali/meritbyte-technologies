## What makes the best website developer in Nepal?

A strong developer can open a live site they built, one that takes enquiries or payments today, on your own phone. After that test, check ten points before signing:

1. **Speed on real connections.** Ask for a past site's Core Web Vitals. The targets are LCP within 2.5 seconds, INP within 200 ms and CLS under 0.1, measured at the 75th percentile of real visits.
2. **Local payments done properly.** They have integrated eSewa, Khalti, Fonepay or ConnectIPS before, and they verify each transaction with the provider's server before marking an order paid.
3. **Nepali in Unicode.** Devanagari text is typed in Unicode, not converted from Preeti, so Google can index it and every phone can display it.
4. **Your name on everything.** The .com.np or .com domain, hosting, Google Business Profile, Facebook page, Search Console and analytics sit under your business login.
5. **A written scope in NPR.** Pages, features, who writes content and whether 13% VAT is included.
6. **Enquiry routes that match how people contact you.** Tap-to-call, WhatsApp and Viber links, Messenger, and a short form.
7. **A maintenance answer.** Who updates plugins, renews the domain and fixes a broken form, and how quickly.
8. **Staging before launch.** You review on a private link, not the live site.
9. **A proper handover.** Documentation, admin training and a list of every credential.
10. **References you can phone.** Owners who will say how the project went.

## Which payment gateways should a Nepali website use?

For customers inside Nepal, offer at least one wallet (eSewa or Khalti) plus Fonepay QR or ConnectIPS. For customers abroad you need card acceptance arranged through a Nepali bank, because Stripe does not onboard Nepal-registered businesses.

| Method | What it is | Where it fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| eSewa | Digital wallet with a web checkout | Retail, fees, tickets, small orders |
| Khalti | Digital wallet with a web checkout | E-commerce, events, subscriptions |
| Fonepay | QR network linking bank and wallet apps | Checkout QR codes, invoices, counter sales |
| ConnectIPS | Direct bank-account payments run by Nepal Clearing House | Tuition, larger orders, B2B |
| Bank card gateway | Visa and Mastercard acceptance through a Nepali bank | Hotel deposits, trekking bookings from abroad |

Each provider needs a merchant account in your registered business name, usually backed by your PAN or VAT certificate, and approval time belongs in the schedule. Payment service providers are licensed by Nepal Rastra Bank. ## What do Nepal's privacy and e-commerce laws mean for your website?

The Individual Privacy Act, 2075 (2018) expects consent and a clear purpose when you collect personal information, so forms should ask only for what you use and the site needs a plain privacy notice. The Electronic Transactions Act, 2063 (2008) gives electronic records and digital signatures legal standing and defines computer-related offences.

For a typical business site:

- Ask for a name and phone number, not a citizenship number, unless a regulation requires it.
- State who sees the data, how long you keep it and how to request deletion.
- Serve every page over HTTPS and put two-factor authentication on admin logins.
- Show your registered business name, PAN or VAT number and contact details.

Nepal has also introduced e-commerce-specific legislation with registration and disclosure duties for online sellers. Confirm the current requirements with the Department of Commerce, Supplies and Consumer Protection or your lawyer before opening a store; a developer can build the disclosures but cannot confirm your registration status.

## How should a Nepali-English website be built?

Publish two complete language versions on separate URLs, such as `/ne/` and `/en/`, written in Unicode and linked with hreflang tags so each page points to its translation. Mixing both languages on a single page leaves Google unsure which searchers it serves.

What separates a careful build from a rushed one:

- **Fonts.** Noto Sans Devanagari and Mukta are free and render conjuncts correctly; test vowel signs on older Android phones.
- **Dates and numbers.** Decide early whether to show Bikram Sambat dates, Gregorian dates or both, and whether prices use Devanagari numerals.
- **Romanised searches.** Plenty of people type Nepali in Latin letters, so romanised terms belong in English-side headings where they read naturally.
- **Domain.** A .com.np domain is free for businesses registered in Nepal and signals a local company; a .com suits firms selling mainly abroad. The guide to [.com.np domain registration](/blog/com-np-domain-registration) lists the documents.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a .com.np domain really free?

Yes. A .com.np domain costs nothing for a business registered in Nepal; you apply through the .np registry with your company registration documents, and approval can take a few days. Make sure the application names your business as the registrant, not your developer or an employee's personal account, because whoever is named controls the domain later.

### Can a Nepali website take payments from customers abroad?

Yes, but not through the wallets, which are built for Nepali users and bank accounts. Foreign customers need card acceptance, which a Nepali business arranges through a bank offering an online card gateway. Trekking companies and hotels often take a card deposit online and the balance on arrival. Stripe does not onboard companies registered in Nepal.

### Should my website be in Nepali, English or both?

It depends on who buys from you. A shop, clinic or school serving local families benefits from Nepali pages; a trekking company, exporter or INGO usually needs English first. Many businesses need both. If you publish both, build complete versions on separate URLs rather than relying on an automatic translation widget, whose output Google does not index as separate pages.

A guesthouse in Pokhara and a shop in Biratnagar would follow the same process and milestones. A person replies to every enquiry within one business day.

### How long does a business website take to build in Nepal?

A brochure site with text and photos ready usually takes four to eight weeks from signed scope to launch. E-commerce and booking sites take longer, mostly because merchant approval from payment providers and preparing product data take time. The quickest way to shorten any project is to have content, photos and prices ready before design starts.
