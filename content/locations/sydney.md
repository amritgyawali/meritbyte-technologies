## Should a Sydney business hire an offshore website developer?

Sometimes. An offshore team makes sense when the budget is fixed, the scope is clear and you are comfortable reviewing work on a staging site and in written updates. It makes less sense when you need someone on site, when a government panel requires an Australian supplier, or when you want to hand over a loose brief and be guided through it in person.

## When are Sydney and Nepal both at work?

Nepal's morning is Sydney's afternoon, all year. The gap changes twice a year because NSW uses daylight saving and Nepal (UTC+5:45) does not:

- **AEST, UTC+10** (first Sunday in April to first Sunday in October): Nepal is 4 hours 15 minutes behind Sydney.
- **AEDT, UTC+11** (first Sunday in October to first Sunday in April): Nepal is 5 hours 15 minutes behind.

| Sydney time | Nepal time during AEST | Nepal time during AEDT |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9:00 am | 4:45 am | 3:45 am |
| 1:15 pm | 9:00 am | 8:00 am |
| 2:30 pm | 10:15 am | 9:15 am |
| 5:00 pm | 12:45 pm | 11:45 am |

If the team in Nepal starts at 9:00 am, calls fit between 1:15 pm and 5:00 pm Sydney time in winter, and between 2:15 pm and 5:00 pm in summer. ## Local search in Sydney is won suburb by suburb

Sydney searchers rarely type just "Sydney". They type the suburb or region: "electrician Northern Beaches", "accountant Parramatta", "physio Surry Hills", "emergency plumber Sutherland Shire". Proximity to the searcher is one of Google's local ranking factors, so in a city this spread out your real competitors are the businesses near the person searching, not the ones across the Harbour Bridge.

What works:

1. A complete Google Business Profile with the right primary category, service areas if you travel to customers, and a steady flow of reviews that you reply to.
2. One page per service, with real detail: typical jobs, response times, licences, and photos of work you have done.
3. Area pages only where you genuinely serve the area and can say something specific about it. Dozens of near-identical suburb pages are what Google's spam policies call doorway pages.
4. Profiles on the platforms Sydney customers compare on, such as hipages or Oneflare for trades, or industry directories for professional firms, each pointing back to your own site.

## What does Australian law expect from a Sydney website?

More than a privacy policy copied from a template. The main obligations:

- **Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles.** These apply to businesses with annual turnover above $3 million and to some smaller ones, including health service providers and businesses that trade in personal information. Amendments passed in late 2024 added a statutory tort for serious invasions of privacy and, from December 2026, require privacy policies to explain certain automated decisions that use personal information.
- **Spam Act 2003.** Commercial email and SMS need consent, must identify the sender and must carry an unsubscribe that takes effect within five working days.
- **Australian Consumer Law.** Consumer-facing prices must show the single total, including GST and compulsory fees. "From $99 + GST" is common in business-to-business quotes but not acceptable as a consumer price.
- **Disability Discrimination Act 1992.** Build to WCAG 2.2 level AA; inaccessible websites can be the subject of complaints under the Act.
- **Domains.** A .com.au needs an Australian presence, usually an ABN or ACN.

Some professions carry an extra layer. NSW restricts how law firms advertise personal injury services, and financial services firms should follow ASIC's guidance on advertising (RG 234). A developer does not need to be your lawyer, but they should ask the question before writing the copy.

## Sydney sectors and what their sites need

### Trades and home services

Quote forms that accept photos, click-to-call on every page, licence and insurance details in plain view, and a link into job management software such as ServiceM8 or Tradify. Many of these searches happen on a phone during an emergency, so mobile speed decides who gets the call.

### Hospitality

Menus as text, reservations through a platform such as OpenTable or SevenRooms, and function pages that capture date, guest numbers and budget in the first enquiry.

### Professional services and startups

Firms in the CBD, North Sydney and Parramatta need authority content, consultation booking and secure document exchange; see [professional services websites](/industries/professional-services). Startups around Tech Central and Surry Hills usually need a Next.js marketing site beside the product, analytics that investors believe, and Stripe billing in the company's own account.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it cheaper to hire a web developer outside Sydney?

Usually, because hourly rates in Nepal and other offshore markets are well below typical Sydney agency rates. The saving only holds if the scope is clear and the work is reviewed properly; a cheap build that has to be redone costs more in the end. Compare quotes against the same written scope and check who will own the code and accounts.

### What time can I talk to a developer in Nepal from Sydney?

Nepal is 4 hours 15 minutes behind Sydney during AEST and 5 hours 15 minutes behind during AEDT. If the Nepal team starts at 9:00 am, calls work from 1:15 pm Sydney time in winter or 2:15 pm in summer, through to 5:00 pm. Written updates and a staging site cover the rest of the day.

### Does my Sydney business website need to comply with the Privacy Act?

If your annual turnover is above $3 million, or you fall into a covered category such as health service providers, yes. Smaller businesses may be exempt, but following the Australian Privacy Principles anyway is sensible: a clear privacy policy, collecting only what you need and secure forms. The Spam Act applies to marketing email whatever your size.

### Do suburb landing pages help Sydney local SEO?

They help when each page is genuinely useful: jobs done in that area, local photos, relevant reviews and accurate service details. Pages that only swap the suburb name are doorway pages under Google's spam policies. For most small businesses, a strong Google Business Profile and one good page per service matter more than dozens of suburb pages.
