## What makes the best website developer in the UAE?

Bilingual competence and local plumbing. Plenty of agencies can make an attractive English site; fewer can ship an Arabic version that feels native and a checkout that works with UAE acquirers. Use this list when comparing them:

1. **Right-to-left from the first line of CSS,** using logical properties so the Arabic layout is designed, not flipped at the end.
2. **Arabic typography chosen for screens,** such as IBM Plex Sans Arabic or Noto Kufi Arabic, paired with a Latin font of similar weight.
3. **Separate Arabic and English URLs** with hreflang tags for ar-AE and en-AE.
4. **The correct data law for your licence:** federal PDPL, DIFC or ADGM.
5. **A UAE gateway** plus Apple Pay, and Tabby or Tamara if your customers expect instalments.
6. **VAT shown properly:** consumer prices include the 5% VAT, and tax invoices carry your Tax Registration Number.
7. **WhatsApp and call buttons** that work on mobile, and forms that accept +971 numbers without errors.
8. **Ownership:** the .ae or .com domain, hosting, gateway merchant account and Meta business assets in your company's name.

## What does a proper Arabic and English build involve?

More than translation. An Arabic version needs its own layout direction, typography, number handling and SEO, planned from the wireframe onwards.

The checklist our designers and developers work through:

- **Direction.** Arabic pages set `dir="rtl"` and `lang="ar"`, and components use start and end rather than left and right so they reverse cleanly.
- **What to mirror.** Arrows, progress steps and carousels reverse; logos, video play buttons, phone numbers and charts usually do not.
- **Typography.** Arabic script needs more line height than Latin, and letter-spacing must stay off because it breaks the joins between letters.
- **Numerals.** Decide whether to use Western digits (0-9) or Arabic-Indic digits, and apply the choice consistently, including in prices and forms.
- **Mixed text.** Brand names, email addresses and product codes inside Arabic sentences need bidirectional isolation, or punctuation lands in the wrong place.
- **Search.** Arabic keyword research is its own job; people search for the same service with different words in each language.

Arabic copy should be written, or at least reviewed, by a native Arabic writer who knows your sector; budget for that separately if you do not have one in-house. ## Which data protection law applies in the UAE?

It depends on where your company is licensed. Most mainland companies and many free zone companies fall under the federal Personal Data Protection Law, Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021. Companies in the Dubai International Financial Centre follow the DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020, and those in Abu Dhabi Global Market follow the ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021.

| Regime | Who it covers | What it changes on the website |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Federal PDPL | Mainland and most non-financial free zones | Clear privacy notice, a lawful basis for processing, conditions on transfers abroad; some detail depends on implementing regulations, so confirm the current position with counsel |
| DIFC Data Protection Law 2020 | Firms licensed in the DIFC | GDPR-style notices and rights, rules on transfers outside the DIFC, a data protection officer for some processing |
| ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021 | Firms registered in ADGM | GDPR-style notices, rights and transfer rules |

Health data carries an extra rule: Federal Law No. 2 of 2019 on the use of ICT in health fields generally requires health data to be stored inside the UAE unless an exception applies. For clinics, that settles the hosting question: use a UAE region, which both AWS and Azure operate. Whatever your regime, the safest arrangement with an overseas developer is test data on staging and no routine access to live customer records.

## Which payment gateways work for UAE websites?

Telr, PayTabs, Network International's N-Genius, Checkout.com and Stripe all accept payments for UAE businesses. The best choice depends on your bank, your sector and whether you need recurring billing, so ask for merchant fees and settlement times from two or three before committing.

A few UAE-specific points:

- Apple Pay is widely used, and adding it usually shortens mobile checkout noticeably.
- Tabby and Tamara offer split payments that shoppers in fashion, electronics and furniture often look for.
- Many stores still offer cash on delivery; if you do, plan for order confirmation by WhatsApp or phone to cut refusals at the door.
- The dirham is pegged to the US dollar at 3.6725, so quotes and costs in either currency stay predictable.
- VAT has been 5% since 2018, and consumer prices should be displayed with it included.

## How closely do Nepal and UAE working hours match?

Almost exactly. The UAE is UTC+4 and Nepal is UTC+5:45; neither uses daylight saving, so the gap is 1h45m all year. A 9 a.m. meeting in Dubai or Abu Dhabi is 10:45 a.m. in Nepal, and a 4 p.m. check-in is 5:45 p.m.

The UAE federal government moved to a Monday-to-Friday working week in 2022, and many private companies followed, so weekly schedules line up well. Plan launches around the calendar that matters locally: working hours shorten during Ramadan, and retail traffic peaks around Ramadan, Eid and the Dubai Shopping Festival, so a new store should be live and tested weeks before those dates, not during them.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you build the Arabic version of my website?

Yes, we build the right-to-left layout, the bilingual structure, hreflang tags and the Arabic versions of forms, emails and checkout. The Arabic copy itself should come from a native writer, either on your team or someone you appoint, because tone and terminology matter to Arabic-speaking customers and machine translation shows.

### Which payment gateway should a UAE business use?

Start with what your bank supports and what your customers use. Telr, PayTabs, Network International and Stripe all serve UAE merchants; compare setup requirements, fees, settlement time and support for Apple Pay and recurring billing. Retailers selling higher-value items should also consider Tabby or Tamara for instalments.

### Does the PDPL apply to my free zone company?

If you are in the DIFC or ADGM, those free zones have their own data protection laws, and they apply instead. Most other free zones, and mainland companies, fall under the federal PDPL. Your licence documents tell you which authority you sit under, and a lawyer can confirm the details for your case.
