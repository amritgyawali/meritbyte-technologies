## Why is Brisbane the simplest Australian city to work with from Nepal?

Because the gap never changes. Queensland voted against daylight saving in a 1992 referendum, so Brisbane stays on AEST, UTC+10, all year. Nepal is UTC+5:45 and has no daylight saving either. The result is a fixed difference: Brisbane is always 4 hours 15 minutes ahead of Nepal.

| Nepal time | Brisbane time, every month |
| --- | --- |
| 9:00 am | 1:15 pm |
| 10:45 am | 3:00 pm |
| 12:45 pm | 5:00 pm |
| 6:00 pm | 10:15 pm |

That gives 3 hours 45 minutes of shared working time every weekday of the year, from 1:15 pm to 5:00 pm in Brisbane, with no twice-yearly recalculation.

One trap catches people out. From the first Sunday in October to the first Sunday in April, Brisbane is an hour behind Sydney and Melbourne. If your calendar invites are set to Sydney time, meetings drift by an hour for half the year. Set your calendar to the Brisbane time zone and the problem disappears. ## Trades and construction: websites that win quotes

Brisbane customers think in Northside and Southside first, then suburbs. A sparky based in Chermside and a plumber in Carindale are rarely chasing the same jobs, and the website should make your patch obvious. What converts for trades and builders:

- a quote form that accepts photos and asks for suburb, job type and timeframe, so you can price without a site visit;
- a page per job type (switchboard upgrades, hot water systems, bathroom renovations) with typical timeframes and what moves the price;
- licence details in plain view: the QBCC has rules on how licensees advertise, so the safe approach is your licensed name and number in the footer and on every service page (check the QBCC's current requirements for your licence class);
- a link to the job management tool your office already uses (ServiceM8, Tradify or simPRO), so web leads do not sit in a separate inbox;
- for commercial builders, a capability statement, project pages with scope and value bands, and WHS and quality certifications, which is what estimators and procurement teams look for.

Pair this with a properly set up Google Business Profile ([our step-by-step guide](/blog/local-seo-google-business-profile)); service-area businesses should hide the home address and list the areas they cover.

## Tourism operators from the river to the coasts

Brisbane is a gateway as much as a destination: visitors land here and head for the Gold Coast, the Sunshine Coast, Moreton Island and the hinterland. Tour and activity operators need:

- live availability and instant confirmation through a booking system such as Rezdy or FareHarbor, embedded so the booking happens on your own site;
- answers to the questions international visitors ask: pickup points in the CBD or South Bank, what to bring, cancellation terms and a weather policy, since summer storms in south-east Queensland are a real reason to reschedule;
- prices in AUD including GST, with any fees shown before payment;
- image-heavy pages that still load quickly on hotel Wi-Fi or roaming data.

## Brisbane 2032: what is worth doing now?

Brisbane was awarded the 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games in 2021. Most of the procurement and the visitor surge is years away, so a "2032-ready" microsite built today is mostly wasted money. What is worth doing now:

1. **Accessibility.** Hosting the Paralympics puts accessible travel in the spotlight, and Australia's landmark web accessibility complaint was about an Olympic website: Maguire v SOCOG, decided in 2000 over the Sydney Games site. Build to WCAG 2.2 AA now and every visitor benefits, whenever they arrive.
2. **Supplier readiness.** If you want Games-related or government work, keep a current capability statement online and register on QTenders, the Queensland Government's tender portal. Government contracts can also bind you, as a contracted service provider, to Queensland's Information Privacy Act 2009 and its Queensland Privacy Principles.
3. **Durable content.** Pages about your venue, tours or services that help visitors now will still help in 2032; event-specific pages can come later.

An honest note: Queensland Government procurement gives weight to local suppliers under its Buy Queensland approach, so a Nepal-based team is rarely the right prime contractor for government work. We are better suited to building the website of the Queensland business that bids.

## What to look for in a web developer in Brisbane

Try a simple test. Give each shortlisted developer this brief: "We are a Northside electrician and we want more switchboard upgrade jobs." The best web developer for a Brisbane business will come back with most of the following without prompting:

1. Questions about which suburbs you want, your margins on that job type, and how you take leads today.
2. A plan for a dedicated service page, Google Business Profile categories and a review routine.
3. Where your licence details will appear, and a note to check the current QBCC rules.
4. How quote requests will reach ServiceM8, simPRO or whatever your office uses.
5. Mobile speed targets (LCP ≤ 2.5 s, INP ≤ 200 ms, CLS ≤ 0.1) and WCAG 2.2 AA.
6. A fixed price for the first stage, and confirmation that the domain, hosting and code will be in your name.
7. A maintenance offer with response times in writing, such as our [managed IT services](/services/managed-it-services).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the time difference between Brisbane and Nepal?

Brisbane is 4 hours 15 minutes ahead of Nepal all year. Queensland does not use daylight saving and neither does Nepal, so the gap never changes. When it is 9:00 am in Nepal it is 1:15 pm in Brisbane, which gives 3 hours 45 minutes of shared working time each weekday afternoon.

### Do Queensland builders have to show their licence on their website?

The QBCC sets advertising rules for licensees, and the safest approach is to show your licensed name and licence number on your website, in the footer and on service pages. Check the QBCC's current guidance for your licence class. Displaying it also reassures customers who check the public licence register before hiring anyone.

### Should my Brisbane business build a website for the 2032 Games now?

Not a dedicated one. The Games are years away and plans will change. Instead, make your current site accessible to WCAG 2.2 AA, keep a capability statement online if you want supply-chain work, and publish content that helps visitors today. Event-specific pages can be added closer to 2032, once schedules are confirmed.

### Is a remote web developer a good fit for a Brisbane tradie?

It can be, if the brief is clear: service pages, a quote form, Google Business Profile and job-software integration can all be done online. It is a poor fit if you want someone to visit your yard, take photos or sit with you every week. Many trades businesses pair a remote build with a local photographer.
