## Why do direct bookings matter so much in Pokhara?

Because OTA commission comes straight off margins that are already seasonal. Hotel commission on the big booking sites typically sits somewhere between 15% and 25%, depending on your contract and any visibility programmes you join, and most of it is paid in the busy months when you might have filled the rooms anyway.

The aim is not to leave the OTAs; they bring guests who would never find you. The aim is to make booking direct the obvious choice for guests who already know your name:

- a direct rate that is at least as good as the OTA rate, plus a perk OTAs cannot show, such as an airport pickup or late check-out (read your OTA contracts for parity clauses first);
- a booking engine with live availability and a card deposit, instead of "email us to check dates";
- Google Hotel free booking links, connected through your booking engine, so your own site appears next to the OTAs in Google's hotel results;
- photos, prices and review replies on your Business Profile that match the website.

The mechanics are covered in our post on [hotel direct bookings in Nepal](/blog/hotel-website-direct-bookings-nepal).

## What a Lakeside hotel or guesthouse site needs

A guest choosing where to stay compares four or five places side by side on a phone, and whichever answers their questions fastest usually wins. Each room page should show:

- real photos of that room, compressed so the page loads on 4G, and the view it actually has: lake, mountain or street;
- bed type, room size, bathroom, hot water and heating details, which matter from December to February;
- walking time to the lakeshore, the main Lakeside strip and the tourist bus park;
- transfer options from Pokhara International Airport, which opened in January 2023 (check which routes are actually scheduled before advertising flights);
- the cancellation policy in one clear paragraph, not buried in terms and conditions.

Payment needs two routes. Foreign guests pay by card through a Nepali bank's gateway, while domestic travellers often prefer eSewa, Khalti or Fonepay QR. Say clearly which currency the card will be charged in, since that depends on how your bank's gateway settles.

Timing matters too. Nepal runs on UTC+5:45, so an enquiry sent at 7 pm from London in summer (BST) lands at 11:45 pm in Pokhara. A booking engine that confirms instantly wins that guest; a form that waits for the morning often does not.

## Paragliding, rafting and trekking operators

Activity businesses sell time slots, and their biggest website problem is weather. A Sarangkot tandem flight or an Upper Seti rafting trip can be called off on the morning, so the booking system has to handle rescheduling, and the policy has to say plainly what happens to the deposit.

Every activity page should answer, in text rather than only in a video: weight and age limits, what to wear, pickup time from Lakeside, total duration, and the operator's permits and pilot or guide certifications.

Pokhara-based trekking companies sell the Annapurna region: Poon Hill, Mardi Himal, Annapurna Base Camp and the Circuit. Itinerary pages need daily altitudes and walking times, permit requirements (the ACAP permit and TIMS) and the guide rules. Since April 2023, foreign trekkers in Nepal's national parks and conservation areas have been required to walk with a licensed guide; because policy and enforcement shift, date the page and review it every season. ## Building around Pokhara's seasons

Pokhara's year has two peaks, autumn (roughly October to November) and spring (March to May), and a monsoon trough from June to September when trekking quietens but domestic travel continues. Plan website work to that rhythm:

| Months | What travellers are doing | What your website work should be |
| --- | --- | --- |
| June to August | Planning autumn trips; domestic holidays | Build or redesign, new photos, content for autumn searches |
| September | Booking autumn, often late | Launch finished, ads switched on, availability accurate |
| October to November | Peak season, Dashain and Tihar | No big changes; answer enquiries fast; collect reviews |
| December to February | Winter trips with clear mountain views | Update winter rates and heating details; plan spring content |
| March to May | Spring peak, rhododendrons in bloom | Keep booking paths fast; gather photos for next year |

Launching a new site in mid-October is the classic timing mistake: you lose the season's learning and risk breaking the booking form when it matters most.

## Which languages should a Pokhara website use?

English first, then the language of your next-largest guest group, done properly. Pokhara draws visitors from China, South Korea, India and Europe as well as from across Nepal. A professionally translated Chinese or Korean version of your top pages (home, rooms or trips, prices, booking) earns more than machine-translating the whole site.

Each language needs its own URLs and hreflang tags so Google shows the right version; our [international SEO guide](/blog/international-seo-guide) explains the setup. Nepali pages help with domestic travellers, who search in both Devanagari and Romanised Nepali.

## Frequently asked questions

### How can a Pokhara hotel get more direct bookings?

Make booking direct easier and slightly better than booking through an OTA. Add a booking engine with live availability and card deposits, offer a perk the OTAs cannot show, connect Google Hotel free booking links, and keep room pages fast with honest photos. Check OTA parity clauses before changing rates, and reply to reviews, which guests read on Google before choosing.

### When is the best time to build a website for a Pokhara business?

During the monsoon months, roughly June to August. Travellers are planning autumn trips then, and a site that is live by early September has time to be indexed and tested before October bookings peak. Avoid big launches in October and November and during the spring peak, when a broken booking form costs real revenue.

### Do Pokhara paragliding and rafting operators need online booking?

Yes, if you sell to independent travellers, who often book on their phone the evening before. The system should handle time slots, weather cancellations and rescheduling, and the policy should state clearly what happens to the deposit. Operators who sell mostly through hotels and agents can start with a strong enquiry form and a WhatsApp link.

### Should my Pokhara website be in Chinese or Korean?

Only if those guests are a meaningful share of your bookings and someone can answer their enquiries. Translate your most important pages professionally rather than machine-translating everything, give each language its own URLs with hreflang tags, and keep prices and policies identical to the English version so nobody books on outdated terms.
