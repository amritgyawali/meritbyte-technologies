## Where does a Shopify developer earn their fee?

Not in installing a theme; most store owners can do that themselves. The value is in the parts that affect margin:

- **Theme architecture.** Online Store 2.0 sections and metafields, so your team can build lookbooks, size charts and collection pages without touching code.
- **Checkout.** Customisations now go through Shopify's Checkout Extensibility (checkout UI extensions and apps) rather than an edited checkout.liquid, so plan what you genuinely need there.
- **Payments.** Shopify Payments, plus Afterpay or Zip, which many Australian fashion shoppers look for at checkout.
- **Shipping.** Australia Post, StarTrack, Sendle or Shippit, with rates that are honest about regional delivery, and Shopify Markets if you ship to New Zealand or the US.
- **Markets and shops.** Shopify POS keeps stock in sync when you sell at a pop-up, a weekend market stall or your own store.
- **Email.** Klaviyo or Shopify Email flows for abandoned carts and post-purchase, built on proper consent.
- **Access.** Your developer should work through a collaborator account, so they never own the store and you can revoke access in one click.

Headless builds on Shopify Hydrogen suit a few content-heavy brands with an in-house team; for most Melbourne labels, a well-built theme is faster to launch and cheaper to run. Our [e-commerce development service](/services/ecommerce-development) covers both routes.

## Planning around Melbourne's event calendar

Melbourne's year has fixed peaks that hit hospitality, retail and ticketing sites alike:

- the Australian Open in January;
- the Formula 1 Australian Grand Prix and the Melbourne Food and Wine Festival in March, with the International Comedy Festival running into April;
- the AFL Grand Final, usually on the last Saturday of September, with a Victorian public holiday on the Friday before;
- the Spring Racing Carnival, ending with the Melbourne Cup on the first Tuesday in November;
- Black Friday, Cyber Monday and Click Frenzy's November sale, then the run to Christmas.

A developer should put these dates on the project plan. The practical rules: no major releases in the fortnight before a known peak, load-test the pages that will take the traffic, and have someone watching the store on the day. Page speed matters most under load; our [Core Web Vitals explainer](/blog/core-web-vitals-explained) sets out the targets.

## Café and restaurant sites that do not fight the delivery apps

Delivery and booking platforms bring customers but take a cut. The website's job is to capture the people who already want you:

- menus published as HTML with prices and dietary tags (vegan, gluten-free), readable by Google and by screen readers, rather than a PDF;
- direct pick-up ordering if your volume justifies it, so regulars stop paying the delivery-app markup;
- bookings through your chosen system, plus a functions form that asks for date, numbers and budget up front;
- hours identical on the site and the Google Business Profile, including Victorian public holidays.

## How do Melbourne and Nepal working hours line up?

Victoria uses daylight saving and Nepal does not, so the gap moves twice a year. From the first Sunday in April to the first Sunday in October, Melbourne is on AEST (UTC+10) and Nepal, on UTC+5:45, sits 4 hours 15 minutes behind. For the rest of the year Melbourne is on AEDT (UTC+11) and the gap widens to 5 hours 15 minutes.

Day to day, a Nepal working day that starts at 9:00 am begins at 1:15 pm Melbourne time in winter and 2:15 pm in summer. Notes you send at 5:00 pm in Melbourne arrive at 12:45 pm in Nepal during AEST, or 11:45 am during AEDT, with most of the Nepal afternoon still ahead. Sydney runs on exactly the same clock as Melbourne, so a team split between the two cities needs no extra planning.

## Consumer law and email rules Melbourne retailers trip over

- **Prices include GST.** Consumer prices must show the total, including GST and compulsory charges. Delivery fees can be shown separately, but they must be clear before payment.
- **Returns.** Consumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law apply whatever your returns page says, so a blanket "no refunds on sale items" can mislead customers about their rights. Write the policy around the change-of-mind returns you choose to offer, plus the guarantees you must honour.
- **Email and SMS consent.** Under the Spam Act 2003, a pop-up that quietly adds everyone to Klaviyo is a risk. Record consent, identify the sender and honour unsubscribes within five working days. See [email marketing for small business](/blog/email-marketing-for-small-business).
- **Privacy.** The Privacy Act 1988 covers businesses turning over more than $3 million, plus some smaller ones. Even under the threshold, the apps on your store collect customer data, so know which ones and why.
- **Accessibility.** WCAG 2.2 AA on the theme, including colour contrast on sale badges and alt text on product photos.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need a Melbourne-based Shopify developer?

Not necessarily. Shopify work happens in the browser, and a remote developer with collaborator access can build and maintain your store from anywhere. A local developer helps if you want in-person workshops or hands-on help at pop-ups. Whoever you hire, keep the store, apps and domain in your name, and have changes tested on an unpublished theme first.

### When should a Melbourne retailer launch a new website?

Away from your peaks. For most retailers that means going live between February and August, leaving September and October to test and tune before Black Friday, Click Frenzy and Christmas. Hospitality venues should also avoid the weeks around their biggest events. Allow at least four weeks between launch and any major sale to catch problems.

### Is Afterpay worth adding to a Melbourne fashion store?

Often, for fashion and homewares where basket sizes suit instalments, because many Australian shoppers look for it at checkout. Its merchant fee is higher than standard card processing, so check it against your margins, and measure whether it actually lifts conversion on your store rather than assuming it will.

### How many hours overlap between Melbourne and a team in Nepal?

With a 9-to-5 day on both sides, about 3 hours 45 minutes during AEST (1:15 pm to 5:00 pm in Melbourne) and 2 hours 45 minutes during AEDT (2:15 pm to 5:00 pm). Afternoon calls work well; everything else runs on written updates and the staging store.
