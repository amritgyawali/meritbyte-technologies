8. **A schedule in your time zone.** Review windows and demo dates written in ET, CT, MT or PT.

## How do US and Nepal working hours line up?

Nepal runs on UTC+5:45 all year with no daylight saving time, so it is 9 hours 45 minutes ahead of US Eastern time in summer and 10 hours 45 minutes ahead in winter. A 9 a.m. meeting in New York is 6:45 p.m. in Nepal between March and November, and 7:45 p.m. in winter.

| US time zone | UTC offset (standard / daylight) | Nepal is ahead by (winter / summer) | 9 a.m. local in Nepal (winter / summer) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Eastern (New York) | −5 / −4 | 10h45m / 9h45m | 7:45 p.m. / 6:45 p.m. |
| Central (Chicago) | −6 / −5 | 11h45m / 10h45m | 8:45 p.m. / 7:45 p.m. |
| Mountain (Denver) | −7 / −6 | 12h45m / 11h45m | 9:45 p.m. / 8:45 p.m. |
| Arizona (most of the state, no DST) | −7 | 12h45m all year | 9:45 p.m. |
| Pacific (Los Angeles) | −8 / −7 | 13h45m / 12h45m | 10:45 p.m. / 9:45 p.m. |

US daylight saving time runs from the second Sunday in March to the first Sunday in November. Nepal never changes its clocks, so the gap shifts by an hour twice a year.

The benefit is the handoff: a Nepal working day of 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. runs from 12:15 a.m. to 8:15 a.m. Eastern daylight time, so feedback sent at 4 p.m. can be picked up and finished before your next morning. ## What do the ADA and WCAG mean for a US business website?

Title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act bars discrimination by businesses open to the public, and plaintiffs routinely apply it to websites; demand letters and settlements usually point to WCAG 2.1 Level AA. No federal regulation sets a technical standard for private business sites, so building to WCAG 2.2 AA and keeping records of testing is the practical defense.

Context worth knowing in 2026:

- The Justice Department's 2024 Title II rule adopted WCAG 2.1 AA for state and local governments. It does not cover private companies, but it shows where regulators set the bar.
- Federal courts disagree on whether a website with no physical location is a place of public accommodation, and a large share of suits are filed in New York; our page for a [web developer in New York](/website-developer/new-york) covers that market.
- In 2025 the FTC finalized an order requiring accessiBe to pay $1 million over claims that its AI tool could make any website WCAG-compliant. An overlay script is not a remediation plan.

In the build, accessibility means semantic HTML, full keyboard access, visible focus states, 4.5:1 contrast for body text, labeled form fields, captions, alt text and an accessibility statement. It costs far less at design stage than as a retrofit.

## Which privacy, email and payment rules touch a US website?

Mostly state privacy laws, federal email and texting rules, and sales tax:

| Rule | When it applies | What the website needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CCPA as amended by CPRA (California) | For-profit businesses over revenue or data-volume thresholds that handle Californians' data | "Do Not Sell or Share" link, Global Privacy Control honored, privacy policy updated at least every 12 months |
| Other state privacy laws (Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas and more) | Thresholds and definitions vary by state | Opt-outs for targeted advertising, consent for sensitive data |
| CAN-SPAM Act | Any commercial email | Your postal address and an unsubscribe honored within 10 business days |
| TCPA | Marketing texts and autodialed calls | Prior express written consent, stored with the phone number |
| Sales tax after *South Dakota v. Wayfair* (2018) | Online sellers past a state's economic nexus threshold | Tax calculated at checkout by state and locality |
| HIPAA | Healthcare providers and vendors handling protected health information | Care with tracking pixels on appointment pages, a business associate agreement with any vendor touching that data |

For payments, Stripe is the usual default on custom builds and Shopify Payments on Shopify stores, with Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal where customers expect them and ACH for larger B2B invoices.
