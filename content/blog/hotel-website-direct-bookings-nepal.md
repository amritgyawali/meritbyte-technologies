A hotel website in Nepal wins direct bookings when a guest can see live availability, book a room at a rate at least as good as on the OTAs, and pay on the spot, whether with a foreign card or with eSewa, Khalti or Fonepay. It also has to be reachable from where guests actually look, which is your Google listing in Search and Maps. A handsome site with only a "contact us" form tends to send guests back to Booking.com or Agoda to finish the booking.

You will not replace the OTAs, and should not try. The realistic goal is to move a share of bookings, especially repeat and domestic guests, to your own channel, where you keep the commission and the relationship.

## What does OTA commission actually cost a Nepali hotel?

More than most owners have calculated. OTA commission is commonly in the region of 15% to 20% of room revenue, depending on the platform, your contract and any visibility programmes you join. On a mid-size property that adds up to lakhs of rupees a year.

An illustrative calculation, with invented but realistic figures:

| Item | Figure |
| --- | --- |
| Rooms | 20 |
| Average room rate | NPR 7,000 |
| Occupancy | 60% |
| Room nights sold a year | 4,380 |
| Share booked through OTAs | 50% (2,190 nights) |
| OTA room revenue | About NPR 1.53 crore |
| Commission at 18% | About NPR 27.6 lakh |

Moving a tenth of those OTA nights to direct bookings would keep roughly NPR 2.8 lakh a year, before payment fees on the direct bookings. Run the sums with your own numbers; they usually justify a proper booking engine.

## What is a hotel booking engine, and which kind do you need?

A booking engine is the software on your website that shows real-time availability and rates and takes reservations. It usually connects to a channel manager, which keeps room inventory in sync across OTAs so the same room is not sold twice, and often to a property management system (PMS) at the front desk.

| Setup | Good for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Booking engine and channel manager from one vendor (for example SiteMinder, Cloudbeds or eZee) | Hotels selling on several OTAs | Monthly fees; check how payments work for a Nepali business |
| Nepal-built hotel systems | Local support, NPR billing, local payment options | Check OTA connections and Google integration |
| WordPress booking plugin | Small guesthouses with few rooms and little OTA use | No inventory sync, so double bookings once OTAs are involved |
| Enquiry form only | Nothing, if the goal is direct bookings | Guests return to the OTA to book |

The question to put to every vendor: how does a guest pay on my site when my hotel is registered in Nepal? Many international engines rely on processors such as Stripe, which does not onboard Nepali businesses. The workable answers are an engine that connects to a Nepali bank's online card gateway, a card guarantee collected through the engine's own tokenised system (never card numbers in your inbox or spreadsheet), or "pay at hotel" backed by a clear cancellation policy.

## Why would a guest book directly instead of on an OTA?

Because you give them a reason, and say so plainly. Guests have learned that OTAs are convenient and usually cheapest; your site has to show otherwise at a glance.

Reasons that work:

- **Best rate on your own site**, stated at the top of the booking engine.
- **Value-adds that cost you little**: breakfast, pickup from Tribhuvan International Airport or Pokhara International Airport, early check-in, late checkout.
- **More flexible cancellation** than the OTA's non-refundable rate.
- **Direct contact**: a WhatsApp or Viber number the front desk actually answers.
- **Packages**: a night before or after a trek, festival stays, honeymoon packages.

Read your OTA contracts before advertising lower prices publicly, since some include rate parity terms. Packages, member rates and value-adds are often a cleaner way to make booking direct the better deal.

## What does a hotel website in Nepal need beyond the booking engine?

A page for each room type, prices without surprises, and the practical information guests check before committing.

- **Room pages** with several photos each, size in square metres, bed configuration, view and amenities.
- **Transparent pricing.** Nepali hotels commonly add a 10% service charge and 13% VAT, with VAT charged on the amount including service charge, so together they add roughly 24% to a base rate. Say clearly whether displayed rates include them.
- **Location and access**: a map, travel time from the airport, road conditions in the monsoon, parking.
- **Policies**: check-in and checkout times, cancellation, children, pets, accepted payment methods.
- **Trust signals**: tourism registration, links to reviews on Google and Tripadvisor, real photos rather than stock.
- **Fast mobile pages.** Compress photos and use a CDN; most guests browse on phones.

## How do Google hotel listings bring direct bookings?

Google shows hotel prices from different sellers in Search and Maps, and a hotel can appear alongside the OTAs with its own direct rate. Google has offered free booking links for hotels, usually connected through booking engines and channel managers that are Google integration partners, so ask your vendor whether it supports them.

Keep your Google Business Profile complete and accurate: category, photos, amenities, check-in times, and a website link that goes to your booking page. Paid metasearch on Google, Tripadvisor or Trivago can follow once the free listings are working.

## How do you win domestic guests?

By meeting them on their own channels. Nepali guests often book by phone, Viber or Facebook message, and pay with eSewa, Khalti or Fonepay QR. Make those options visible and the website becomes the place where domestic guests check details and pay.

- Accept Nepali wallets and Fonepay QR, with payments verified automatically where possible; see our [eSewa, Khalti and Fonepay integration guide](/blog/esewa-khalti-fonepay-integration).
- Offer Nepali-language pages if many guests are domestic, written in Unicode.
- Publish packages for public holidays and festivals, when domestic travel peaks.
- Quote corporate and group rates for conferences, weddings and events.

## How do you know whether direct bookings are growing?

Measure them. Set up Google Analytics 4 with cross-domain tracking to your booking engine, so completed bookings and their value are recorded against the source that brought the guest. Add UTM tags to your Google Business Profile link. Each month, review three numbers: the direct share of room nights, OTA commission paid, and the cost of winning direct bookings (payment fees and ads). The direct share is the one to push up.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which booking engine is best for a small hotel in Nepal?

The one that syncs with the OTAs you already use, supports a payment method your Nepal-registered business can actually receive, and offers support during your working hours. For properties on several OTAs, a combined booking engine and channel manager avoids double bookings. Ask vendors for a demo using your own room types and make a test booking from start to finish.

### Can a hotel stop using Booking.com entirely?

Rarely, and trying is usually a mistake. OTAs bring international guests who would not otherwise find you. A better aim is balance: keep OTAs for reach, and move repeat guests, domestic guests and people who find you on Google to direct bookings, where you keep the commission and the relationship.

### Can a hotel website in Nepal accept foreign credit cards?

Yes, through a Nepali bank that offers online card acquiring, either connected to your booking engine or as a separate payment page. Stripe does not onboard Nepal-registered businesses, so international booking engines that depend on it need another payment option. Talk to your bank about its e-commerce card service and its requirements early, because approval takes time.
